# Computed Light: light-field photo viewers

- `living-pictures/`: tap to refocus, with a depth-of-field slider
- `light-field/`: drag or tilt to look around, and refocus with light-field math (Stanford Lytro archive)

## Adding your own photos later
Each viewer reads its photos from `scenes.json` and the `scenes/` folder next to it.
Ask Claude to rebuild those from new Lytro exports, then replace the two items and upload again.
