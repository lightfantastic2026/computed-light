How it works

A light-field camera puts thousands of tiny lenses in front of its sensor, so one shot records the scene from 196 slightly different spots inside the lens. This page uses 64 of them.

Drag or tilt moves your viewpoint between them, so near things slide past far ones. Tap lines all the viewpoints up at that distance and averages them: what's there stays sharp and everything else blurs. Deep depth of field uses fewer viewpoints, like stopping down a lens. It's computed live on your phone.
