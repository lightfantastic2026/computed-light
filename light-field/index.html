How it works

Look around shows one of 64 viewpoints from inside the lens opening. They're only millimeters apart, so near things slide against far things, the way they do when you move your head.

Refocus lines the viewpoints up for a chosen distance and averages them. Whatever sits at that distance stays sharp; everything else blurs away. Use more viewpoints and the "aperture" opens wider. That's real light-field math, done after the photo was taken.
