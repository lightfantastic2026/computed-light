How it works

A light-field camera puts thousands of tiny lenses in front of its sensor, so it records which direction each ray of light came from. Software uses that to rebuild the picture focused at any distance after the shot. Here you're flipping between versions it rendered.

The depth-of-field slider goes one step further: it focus-stacks neighboring versions, keeping the sharpest parts of each, until everything from front to back is in focus.
